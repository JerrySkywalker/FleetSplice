## Findings

### High

1. **AuthorityAnchor rollover summaries make the successor genesis digest
   self-referential.** The ADR-0001, ADR-0002, ADR-0004, and authority-model
   summaries say the immutable `SuccessorGenesisCore` contains the full
   successor tuple, or the old and successor tuples. In those same documents,
   the defined lineage tuple contains `genesisDigest`. They then derive that
   digest from the core plus the exact old terminal receipt/link. Read
   literally, the input core must therefore contain the digest whose derivation
   depends on that core. The detailed normative baseline is already correct: it
   enumerates the successor inputs without including the derived
   `genesisDigest`.

   The bounded correction must change only the summaries' recursive shorthand.
   They must bind the full old tuple and the complete successor tuple inputs,
   explicitly excluding the derived successor `genesisDigest`. Those inputs
   include the same `fleetId`, fresh `anchorId`, successor `trustRootDigest`,
   monotonic `epoch`, receipt-verification material, closed writer registry and
   scopes, lifecycle-writer authorization, custody/mechanism/pin/lifecycle
   policy, predecessor closure, and all other fixed identifiers, randomness,
   and configuration. The successor `genesisDigest` is derived exactly once
   from that immutable core plus the exact authenticated old terminal
   receipt/link. No field may circularly include it and no alternate derivation
   is allowed. References:
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:67-68,89-102`;
   `docs/adr/0002-session-identity-control-and-authority.md:42-43,61-69`;
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:145-146,164-175`;
   `docs/architecture/authority-model.md:22-24,45-53`; and, as the correct
   normative contrast, `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:227-249`.

### Medium

1. **Safety activation incorrectly waits for the selected productive
   boundary's outcome before delivering the exact reduction-only control.** An
   exact selected productive boundary may remain
   `CONSUMED_EFFECT_POSSIBLE`, pending, or `AMBIGUOUS_EFFECT` and be the very
   operation that requires interrupt or cancellation. The current activation
   text nevertheless bars SafetyControl delivery until that outcome is
   reconciled. That contradicts the exact-target exception and the rule that
   safety does not wait for productive drain.

   Identity or reservation-map ambiguity must continue to block activation.
   Once the immutable local latches, authoritative companion cut, and complete
   gap-free Edge consistency map establish one exact selected identity without
   a gap, fork, rollback, mismatch, or extra consume, the bound reduction-only
   SafetyControl may activate and its unique delivery owner may deliver even
   while that selected productive outcome remains pending, effect-possible, or
   ambiguous. Activation and receipts must bind separate classifications for
   the productive boundary and the safety-delivery attempt. Safety delivery
   must not infer productive outcome, target termination, rollback, or barrier
   completion; the selected productive boundary and its aliases remain
   quarantined until independently reconciled. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:1042-1047,1062-1077`;
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:433-459`;
   `docs/adr/0002-session-identity-control-and-authority.md:172-190`;
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:313-341`; and
   `docs/architecture/authority-model.md:96-104`.

### Low

1. **ADR-0006 overbroadly rejects a reused rollover transition identity.** Its
   current statement that a reused transition rejects conflicts with the
   required exact-ID crash and ambiguity retry. The bounded correction must say
   that reuse with a changed core, link, receipt, or other bound field rejects,
   while a byte-identical replay of the same transition identity returns the
   existing receipt and never appends or creates an alternate genesis.
   References:
   `docs/adr/0006-security-provenance-and-self-iteration.md:55-58`.

## Finding

### Medium

1. **SafetyControl activation binds a delivery result that can exist only after
   activation.** The immutable `SafetyControlActivation` currently binds
   `safetyDeliveryClassification`, but only the later `DELIVERY_OWNER` gate CAS
   and delivery/outcome evidence can establish that classification. The current
   contract does not define `UNDELIVERED` as a delivery classification. The
   activation must therefore predict the later result, be rewritten after the
   effect boundary, or carry an undefined initial value. Each alternative
   violates the closed late-binding and immutable activation rules.

   The bounded correction must make activation bind the selected productive
   boundary classification and its exact outcome snapshot, together with the
   immutable delivery owner, gate, route, native/process identity, closed
   action, one-use delivery slot, and explicit initial delivery state
   `UNDELIVERED`. Activation must neither bind nor predict the resulting
   `safetyDeliveryClassification`. Only the later immutable delivery or outcome
   receipt under that same one-use slot establishes the result. Immediately
   before first native emission, the delivery-owner CAS records
   `DELIVERY_EFFECT_POSSIBLE`. A qualified no-effect, already-terminal,
   unsupported, or later ambiguous outcome appends its own immutable evidence
   under the same slot without rewriting candidate, activation, or earlier
   receipts. Exact replay returns the applicable receipt, never re-emits, and
   never rewrites or chooses a different result. All unique-owner, no-fallback,
   immediate-latch, exact-selected-pending-boundary delivery, quarantine, and
   no-outcome/termination/rollback/barrier-inference rules remain unchanged.

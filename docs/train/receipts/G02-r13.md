## Finding

### Medium

1. **SafetyControl outcome receipts do not have one canonical ordered delivery
   lineage.** The candidate and activation precommit only the one-use
   `controlDeliverySlotId`. The initial delivery CAS is unique, but a later
   `SafetyControlOutcomeReceipt` merely appends under that slot and receipt
   lineage. The contract does not precommit a stable identity for each later
   stage, require an exact predecessor stage and head digest, CAS against one
   current delivery-lineage head at the single delivery gate, define a closed
   monotonic transition table, or reject competing results. A qualified result
   and `AMBIGUOUS_EFFECT` can therefore fork from the same delivery receipt, and
   exact replay cannot determine one authoritative current state.

   The bounded correction must make the candidate precommit a finite ordered
   delivery-stage plan with stable initial-delivery and outcome receipt IDs and
   slots, closed schemas, and, if ambiguity resolution is allowed, a separate
   precommitted ambiguity-resolution stage and identity. Activation binds only
   those identities and schemas, never a resulting classification. Every later
   delivery or outcome receipt must exact-predecessor CAS the current stage and
   head digest at the single delivery gate, leaving exactly one current head.
   Byte-identical exact-ID replay returns the existing receipt and state without
   an append or native re-emission; a changed tuple, changed predecessor, or
   competing sibling result rejects without effect. Only explicitly listed
   monotonic next-stage transitions are valid, and ambiguity may be resolved
   only through its separately precommitted next stage rather than by rewriting
   or forking an earlier receipt.

   This correction must preserve the explicit `UNDELIVERED` activation state,
   unique `DELIVERY_OWNER`, `FENCE_ONLY` peers, no fallback, no replay emission,
   exact selected productive-boundary safety delivery while its outcome remains
   pending/effect-possible/ambiguous, and quarantine until independent
   reconciliation. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:737-753,975-1000,1071-1087,1125-1139`;

## Findings

### Medium

1. **Round 14 coherently closes O, but D and R still use generic qualified
   evidence instead of a closed result-row evidence contract.** O now binds
   stable result-row IDs, exact eligible writer modes, a closed evidence union,
   noncircular result-specific predicates, candidate-bound producer selectors,
   and exact predecessor, eligibility, and tombstone effects into the
   transition-table digest. D still allows generic "qualified pre-emission
   evidence" to select among its distinct results, and R still allows generic
   "qualified evidence" to select either resolution. The plan therefore does
   not yet make every D and R result mechanically exclusive to its own evidence
   predicate and qualified producer.

   The bounded correction must extend the same stable result-row/evidence
   machinery to every D and R result: stable row IDs; exact eligible writer and
   writer mode; a closed evidence-union arm; a result-specific, noncircular
   predicate; a candidate-bound qualified independent producer selector and
   immutable producer revision; and exact predecessor, resulting eligibility,
   and tombstones covered by the transition-table digest. Every R row must bind
   `D*`, the exact ambiguous O receipt and head, delivery owner/incarnation,
   target action and final native boundary, and runtime, helper, and capability
   revisions. Qualified evidence of crossing or irrevocable acceptance may map
   only to `RESOLVED_DELIVERY_EFFECT_POSSIBLE`; complete evidence that the exact
   boundary did not cross in the past, together with the future-emission fence,
   may map only to `RESOLVED_NO_DELIVERY_EFFECT`. The R writer cannot
   self-attest either predicate. D likewise requires a distinct result-specific
   pre-emission predicate for every result row.

   This correction must preserve the finite precommitted D/O/R chain, D's
   unique one-shot emission authority, no re-emission, exact-predecessor CAS,
   one non-forking head, closed results, atomic tombstones, no fourth or dynamic
   stage, and exclusion of future selected rows, evidence values, receipts, and
   digests from the candidate and activation. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:1011-1048,1131-1259,2032-2058`.

2. **The G03 procedure can declare an unreviewed promotion head accepted.** Its
   current sequence obtains an independent content review at head A, then sets
   `ARCHITECTURE_0_1_READY=true` in the baseline and changes `AGENTS.md` at a new
   head B before creating the acceptance receipt. Because B is not itself
   independently reviewed, the literal commit later advertised as accepted is
   not the exact head/tree that received the PASS. This violates the exact-head
   architecture gate even if the post-review edits appear mechanical.

   After a content PASS, G03 must create one enumerated promotion commit
   containing the authorized readiness-baseline and `AGENTS.md` changes, then
   obtain a fresh independent review of that promotion commit's exact SHA and
   tree. Only after that exact promotion head passes may a receipt-only
   follow-up publish its literal accepted SHA, tree, and baseline path. Any
   other change to the promotion head invalidates that review and requires a
   fresh exact-head/tree review. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:1930-1944` and
   `goals/FLEETSPLICE-ARCH-0_1-ACCEPTANCE-003.md:5-14`.

## Findings

### Medium

1. **D's four individually evidenced rows are not mechanically exclusive at
   one authoritative state cut.** The terminal, canceled, unsupported, and
   final-gate-readiness predicates can coexist or race because their facts may
   come from separate producers and times. Although each row now has a stable
   ID, producer selector and revision, and result-specific predicate, the plan
   does not require one complete classification source set or evaluate every
   predicate against the same state vector at the serialized final gate. A D
   writer can therefore receive more than one apparently valid row or select a
   readiness row while a terminal, cancellation, or unsupported fact races it.

   The bounded correction must define one candidate-precommitted complete
   classification-source manifest and an authoritative cut or high-water at the
   serialized final gate. All D rows must be evaluated against that same
   complete state vector under total precedence and explicit negative guards;
   zero or multiple matching rows must reject without changing `S0` or granting
   emission. The classification schema, complete source-set identity, cut,
   precedence, negative guards, and zero/multiple-match rejection must be bound
   into the transition-table digest. This must preserve the rule that only the
   successful effect-possible D CAS grants its exact winner/incarnation one
   emission. References: `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:1040-1050,1199-1231,1288-1308,1335-1342`.

2. **R's no-effect predicate leaves an emission interval between its no-past
   prefix and its future fence.** Complete reconciliation to a route, journal,
   or native-boundary prefix proves only that no crossing was visible through
   that prefix. Separately obtaining `F(D*)` proves only that `OW` cannot emit
   after the fence. The current contract does not causally close the interval
   between those events, so `OW` may emit after the reconciled prefix and before
   the fence while both required artifacts later appear valid.

   The bounded correction must precommit a finite, complete manifest of every
   authoritative route, journal, and native source plus the exact fence slot.
   `F(D*)` must close the serialized final gate before the terminal no-past cut,
   or both operations must form one atomic composite. The no-effect evidence
   must bind each source's base and final high-water, predecessor digests, the
   complete source-set digest, the fence receipt and slot, and the causal order
   from fence closure through the final no-past cut. A missing source, gap,
   fork, rollback, mismatch, or unclosed interval remains ambiguous and cannot
   select `RESOLVED_NO_DELIVERY_EFFECT`. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:1052-1079,1263-1285,1327-1342`.

3. **The G03 promotion allowlist excludes two documents whose current wording
   would contradict an accepted Architecture 0.1 tree.** Both
   `docs/architecture/history-and-handoff.md` and
   `docs/architecture/host-runtime-model.md` describe Baseline 0.1 or its
   architecture selections as `Proposed`. G03 would promote the baseline and
   ADRs to accepted while forbidding changes outside its exact allowlist, but
   neither document is in that allowlist. The supposedly accepted tree could
   therefore retain contradictory current-state wording, while changing it
   would itself invalidate promotion.

   Before a content PASS, either add both paths to the exact promotion allowlist
   with changes restricted to the same current-readiness wording, or make their
   wording readiness-neutral while the architecture is still a draft. The
   promotion review must then verify the complete closed diff at its literal
   SHA and tree. References:
   `docs/architecture/history-and-handoff.md:13-20,52-58`,
   `docs/architecture/host-runtime-model.md:21-35`,
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:2040-2095`, and
   `goals/FLEETSPLICE-ARCH-0_1-ACCEPTANCE-003.md:5-60`.

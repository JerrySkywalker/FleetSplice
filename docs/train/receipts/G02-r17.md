## Finding

### Medium

1. **D calls four partial row predicates exhaustive even though a complete,
   consistent supported/live/nonterminal vector can match none.** The terminal
   row does not match a nonterminal target; the canceled row does not match a
   live attempt; the unsupported row does not match supported capability; and
   the effect-possible row additionally requires the final gate, owner,
   bindings, and qualifications to be current and `READY`. A nonterminal target
   with a live attempt and supported capability therefore matches no row when
   one of those final-gate conditions is noncurrent or not `READY`.

   That zero-match result is safely rejected by the written fail-closed rule,
   but it contradicts the same text's claim that the four predicates are
   exhaustive and that every complete consistent vector selects exactly one
   row. Qualification also enumerates current/noncurrent and ready/not-ready
   combinations while requiring every such complete consistent vector to
   derive exactly one row, then separately requires zero-match cases to reject.
   A conforming implementation and test oracle cannot satisfy all of those
   claims simultaneously. The contradiction appears in all five normative and
   qualification mirrors. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:1096-1107,1318-1322,2313-2321`,
   `docs/architecture/authority-model.md:259-272,331-334`,
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:608-622,747-750`,
   `docs/adr/0002-session-identity-control-and-authority.md:335-348,464-467`,
   and
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:482-495,682-685`.

   The bounded correction must preserve the four stable D rows and the current
   fail-closed behavior. It must define the row-eligible/classifiable domain,
   state that the four predicates are mutually exclusive and select at most one
   row, and make supported/live/nonterminal but noncurrent or not-`READY`
   final-gate input an explicit zero-match case that seals `SEALED_REJECTED`,
   leaves `S0` unchanged, and grants no emission. Replace the exhaustive/every-
   complete-consistent-vector claims and align the normative and qualification
   wording in all five mirrors. Qualification must distinguish one-row
   classifiable vectors from zero-match rejected vectors. Keep zero/multiple-
   match rejection and its classifier/transition-table digest binding intact.

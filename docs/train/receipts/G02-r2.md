## Combined supervisor finding set

The two independent audits both returned `CHANGE_REQUIRED` but differed on the
sufficiency of some clauses. This receipt preserves that disagreement by
recording the supervisor-accepted conservative union; it does not present every
finding as unanimous.

### High

1. **Recovery generation is absent from command, resolution, Edge replay, and
   anchored composite completeness identities.** The restore rule defines a
   monotonic `recoveryGeneration`, but a client FleetCommand does not precondition
   the expected Hub recovery generation and the frozen plan/EdgeCommand/replay
   tuple does not bind exact Hub and target-Edge recovery generations. A restore
   could therefore admit a pre-recovery identity or trust a rolled-back
   database's incomplete receipt/tombstone/step manifest. The external anchor or
   full-reenrollment path must monotonically fence every pre-restore command,
   plan, step, stream, stale instance, and aggregate manifest even when the
   restored databases lack later records; no new dispatch may occur before
   monotonic proof and reconciliation. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:510-519` versus FleetCommand/plan/Edge
   identity at `:224-286`, ordered plan manifest at `:242`, and
   `docs/architecture/domain-model.md:102`.

### Medium

2. **NativeSegment contradictorily binds ephemeral runtime instances.** A
   NativeSegment is a durable binding/native-identity epoch, but the session
   model says every segment binds current runtime-instance identities. Runtime
   restart may reattach to the same segment only after qualified reconciliation
   proves the same native/process identity and unchanged durable binding and
   generations, recorded as an append-only `RuntimeAttachment` transition.
   Otherwise the state is unknown/lost/ambiguous and needs resolution or a new
   segment. References: `docs/architecture/session-model.md:34` versus
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:147,182` and
   `docs/architecture/domain-model.md:65`.
3. **Composite aggregate outcome rules are omitted.** The draft freezes step
   identities and stores an ordered manifest but does not normatively say that
   aggregate `SUCCEEDED` requires every required step to succeed or that mixed
   outcomes are `PARTIAL_EFFECT` or `AMBIGUOUS_EFFECT`. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:242-258` and
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:28-42` versus
   `docs/research/wave-02/fleet-command-to-hcp.md:145-166`.
4. **G06 is assigned browser-reopen and durable-history behavior owned by G07
   and G08.** G06 adds the exact two-host path plus Host/Environment reconnect
   identity; G07 owns browser close/reopen projection recovery without a
   duplicate effect, and G08 owns durable Fleet history plus Hub/Edge/native
   disruption recovery. References: `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:573-575`,
   `docs/architecture/webui-model.md:269-272`, and
   `docs/architecture/webui-wireframes.md:443-446` versus
   `goals/FLEETSPLICE-V0_1-M1-TWO-HOST-LOOP-006.md:9-14,22-30`,
   `goals/FLEETSPLICE-V0_1-M2-DAILY-CONTROL-007.md:15-25`, and
   `goals/FLEETSPLICE-V0_1-M3-DURABLE-SESSION-008.md:5-26`.
5. **The roadmap retains the obsolete confirmed-migration-only wording.** It
   must match the singular two-outcome G09 gate: qualified owner-confirmed
   activation or a verified, visible, fail-closed `NO_QUALIFIED_TARGET`, with no
   transparent failover. References:
   `docs/roadmap/full-development-train.md:24` versus root Goal `:110-112`,
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:600-606`, G04 `:35-39`, and G09
   `:25-29`.

## Findings

### High

1. **An activated permit's horizon can outrun the acknowledged recovery
   anchor.** The current draft commits and acknowledges an initial plan anchor,
   then creates `DispatchPermit` and `effectLeaseNotAfter` evidence. It does not
   require the exact candidate permit and its horizon to be synchronously
   committed outside rollback domains and acknowledged by every target Edge
   before authenticated activation or release makes that permit effect-bearing.
   In particular, a newer, renewed, replacement, or later composite-step permit
   can be released without a new acknowledged anchor record for its exact
   identity and horizon. The externally recorded maximum horizon can therefore
   lag an already activated permit, reopening rollback overlap across later
   effects even though first dispatch was anchored. Every permit must be fully
   formed before activation, bound to exact command/plan/step, recovery,
   resource, timer, deadline, budget, and uncertainty evidence, and either be
   acknowledged exactly before release or be covered by a pre-anchored immutable
   maximum. There can be no asynchronous anchor lag across any effect.
   References: `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:332-368,430-436,602-641`;
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:50-68,91-102`;
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:38-68`.

2. **Monotonic authority transitions can become usable or externally final
   before rollback-resistant acknowledgement.** The anchor coverage names grant
   issuance/revocation, lane epochs/revisions, resource-generation high-water
   marks, recovery generations, and tombstones, but the ordering rule gates that
   coverage only before the first Edge effect for a resolution. It does not
   require each later authority transition to be fully formed and synchronously
   anchor-committed and acknowledged before success or terminal publication or
   before the transition authorizes an effect. Rollback can therefore lose an
   externally observed grant issuance, revocation, controller fence, resource
   generation, or recovery-generation advance and then reuse or resurrect the
   superseded authority. Pending issuance, control, or generation state must be
   unusable. Revocation must begin local fail-closed quiescence immediately,
   while its affected scope stays blocked and no terminal revocation claim is
   published until the exact fence/tombstone is anchor-acknowledged. A crash or
   ambiguous acknowledgement must retain quarantine and retry only the exact
   idempotency identity. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:332-368,443-488,602-648`;
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:50-68,91-102`;
   `docs/adr/0002-session-identity-control-and-authority.md:26-61`;
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:38-68`.

### Medium

3. **Lease expiry lacks a fail-closed monotonic remaining-budget and timer-epoch
   contract.** The draft binds an absolute `effectLeaseNotAfter` and declared
   clock/skew bound, while the research contract says HCP converts a Hub deadline
   to a bounded remaining budget. The normative baseline and ADRs do not require
   the Edge to derive and persist the tighter local monotonic deadline, bind it
   to the exact Edge boot/timer epoch, or invalidate the permit when clock
   rollback, excessive or unknown uncertainty, suspend/resume, sleep/hibernate,
   reboot, monotonic reset, or lost timer provenance makes safe expiry
   unprovable. Nor does the time-only Hub restore path require trusted time
   continuity and uncertainty proof before relying on the anchored maximum
   horizon. An interrupted or uncertain lease must never be extended; loss of
   proof must require current-generation resynchronization and a freshly
   anchored permit, or per-Edge acknowledged quiescence and reconciliation with
   unreachable Edges quarantined. References:
   `docs/research/wave-02/fleet-command.md:197-205`;
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:352-368,426-436,602-641`;
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:61-68,91-102,118-119`;
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:48-68,83-89`.

## Finding

### High

1. **Ordinary Host, Environment, or Workspace generation replacement lacks a
   predecessor no-overlap/successor-activation barrier.** Anchor acknowledgement
   now prevents rollback of the successor generation, but the successor can
   become current and effect-usable immediately afterward. A disconnected
   predecessor may not yet have observed the replacement and may still drain an
   already activated permit within its valid witnessed monotonic lease. The
   draft applies a no-overlap activation/quiescence barrier after Hub restore,
   but not to ordinary reenrollment, identity/configuration replacement, or an
   Edge-owned Workspace resolved-root generation change. New and predecessor
   generations can therefore cross potentially conflicting effect boundaries at
   the same time even though both follow their individual permit and anchor
   rules.

   Every Host, Environment, and Workspace generation replacement needs one
   named predecessor no-overlap/successor-activation barrier. Anchor
   acknowledgement is necessary but not sufficient: the successor may be
   visible as pending, but cannot become current/usable or authorize or activate
   a potentially conflicting permit until either every affected predecessor has
   observed the successor fence, durably acknowledged old-generation admission
   closure, quiesced, and reconciled final effect/journal/receipt/tombstone
   boundaries, or trusted time-continuity evidence proves the current time is
   past every externally anchor-acknowledged maximum predecessor permit/deadline
   horizon plus uncertainty margin while unreachable predecessors remain
   quarantined. Without trustworthy time proof, or for a family without
   enforceable lease-end quiescence, only the acknowledgement/reconciliation
   path is valid.

   A predecessor may reconnect or re-enter only after observing the current
   generation and reconciling before any effect. Workspace replacement requires
   the equivalent Edge-local closure and reconciliation of its old path,
   process, native, journal, receipt, tombstone, and effect boundaries. Permit
   activation must verify the exact barrier proof and current resource
   generation. Proven-disjoint work remains available; this is not authority for
   placement, a scheduler, or general DAG orchestration. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:120,158-174,331-340,343-431,698-748`;
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:50-111,126-160`;
   `docs/adr/0002-session-identity-control-and-authority.md:26-42`;
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:38-103`;
   `docs/research/wave-01/host-control-protocol.md:7-13,67,91-101,119,153`;
   `docs/research/wave-02/fleet-command-to-hcp.md:84-100,102-143`.

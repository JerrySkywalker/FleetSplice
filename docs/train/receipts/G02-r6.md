## Findings

### High

1. **`PredecessorNoOverlapBarrier` successor coverage is incomplete for
   Edge-only recovery and same-generation effect-capable runtime
   reincarnation.** These are two manifestations of one invariant: no successor
   identity may authorize effects that can overlap a predecessor unless
   predecessor termination or exclusive ownership is durably proved, or the
   existing no-overlap barrier completes.

   The current named barrier is expressly defined for Host, Environment, and
   Workspace generation successors, while the concrete recovery procedure is
   entered only after Hub restore and permit proofs are described in terms of
   resource-generation successors. An Edge authority-store restore can
   therefore advance from an old to a new `edgeRecoveryGeneration` without
   changing Host, Environment, or Workspace generation, then activate a
   conflicting permit while an old Edge/runtime/native effect remains possible.
   This contradicts the requirement that Edge recovery reconcile
   admitted/in-progress journal rows against exact process/native identities
   before retry.

   Ordinary OS, Edge, or companion restart likewise changes `hostBootId`,
   `edgeInstanceId`, `environmentInstanceId`, `edgeTimerEpoch`, stream, or
   managed/native attachment identity without necessarily changing a durable
   resource or recovery generation. Rejecting an old stream is not durable proof
   that its effect-capable predecessor terminated. A replacement runtime can
   therefore become effect-bearing under unchanged durable generations while
   predecessor work is still possible.

   The bounded correction must generalize the existing
   `PredecessorNoOverlapBarrier`, not create a parallel model. It applies to any
   successor identity capable of conflicting with a predecessor whose
   termination or exclusive ownership is unproved: Host/Environment/Workspace
   generation successors; Hub or Edge recovery-generation successors after
   either authority-store restore; and replacement effect-capable runtime
   incarnations in the same scope. The proof binds typed exact
   predecessor/successor identity tuples, conflict scope, affected participants,
   anchor and horizon evidence, and reconciliation evidence. A successor may
   perform non-effecting observation and reconciliation, but remains
   effect-inactive until the proof is anchor-acknowledged.

   The acknowledged-quiescence path must also admit qualified durable proof of
   predecessor nonexistence or exclusive termination plus complete
   journal/process/native/effect reconciliation, so a proven clean restart need
   not wait for horizon expiry. Socket or stream loss, PID reuse, and
   unqualified absence are insufficient. The trusted-time path remains bounded
   by every anchored predecessor horizon plus margin and unreachable-predecessor
   quarantine. Every successor permit and activation binds and rechecks all
   applicable resource, recovery, and runtime barrier proofs. Proven-disjoint
   identities and scopes remain available; this adds no scheduler or placement
   authority. References: `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:140-253,357-485,736-811`;
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:50-163`;
   `docs/adr/0002-session-identity-control-and-authority.md:26-70`;
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:38-125`;
   `docs/architecture/domain-model.md:74-82,140-155`;
   `docs/architecture/session-model.md:35-45`;
   `docs/research/wave-01/host-control-protocol.md:67,87-101,111-119`;
   `docs/research/wave-02/storage-qualification.md:163-165`.

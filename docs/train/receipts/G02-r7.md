## Findings

### High

1. **The admin companion does not enforce the complete activated
   `DispatchPermit` and transitive successor proof at its privileged effect
   boundary.** The general Edge boundary requires an immutable candidate,
   anchor acknowledgement, Edge acknowledgement, authenticated activation,
   durable activation receipt, current generation/runtime evidence, and every
   applicable specialized fence, no-overlap barrier, or exact-disjointness
   proof. The separately trusted admin companion instead validates a live Hub
   decision snapshot, command family, short-lived grant, human confirmation,
   caller identity, nonce, and allowlist, but it does not explicitly require and
   recheck the full activated permit and transitive barrier set immediately
   before each privileged effect. A stale or incompletely fenced Edge request
   can therefore satisfy the narrower companion list even though the generic
   effect permit would fail closed. The bounded correction must make the admin
   companion an independent verifier of the same complete activated permit and
   successor evidence, additionally constrained by its admin-specific checks;
   neither layer may substitute for the other. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:495-565,730-767` and
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:21-108`.

### Medium

1. **Risk-reducing interrupt and cancellation can be circularly blocked by the
   general successor barrier.** The draft permits a separately authorized exact
   safety interrupt, but the generalized trigger can require predecessor
   quiescence and final-boundary reconciliation before any overlapping
   authority successor becomes effect-active. When interrupt or cancellation
   is itself the operation required to make that predecessor quiesce, applying
   the ordinary effect-activation rule first creates a deadlock. The bounded
   correction must define a narrowly non-expansive, exact-predecessor safety
   path that may only reduce or terminate already admitted effects, carries its
   own authority and immutable receipt, cannot start replacement work or widen
   scope, and feeds its result back into the same barrier proof. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:624-692` and
   `docs/adr/0002-session-identity-control-and-authority.md:90-103`.

2. **The same-executor renewal ordering is cyclic.** A successor permit
   candidate must already bind every applicable specialized-fence receipt before
   the candidate is anchored, while the same-executor renewal path relies on an
   atomic Edge-journal supersession of the predecessor and transfer to that
   successor. If the fence receipt is produced only by superseding to the new
   permit, its identity and acknowledged anchor do not yet exist; if
   supersession waits for the anchored successor, the candidate cannot contain
   the required prior receipt. The bounded correction must split this into
   non-effecting preparation and an exact, crash-safe activation sequence whose
   identities are known without self-reference, whose anchor ordering cannot
   lag any effect authority, and whose ambiguity remains quarantined rather
   than leaving two permits usable. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:495-565` and
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:113-150`.

## Finding

### Medium

1. **A delayed or replayed predecessor privileged-boundary request can cross at
   the companion during a partial Edge/companion permit transfer.** The new
   renewal protocol correctly makes each local `X_i` atomic and says a partial
   multi-participant switch is safe unavailability. The admin protocol,
   however, lets the Edge journal an activation receipt and transmit a
   privileged request that the companion later verifies using caller identity,
   replay nonce, its current local state, and permit evidence. Those fields do
   not define an immutable one-use effect-boundary reservation that is consumed
   atomically at the companion, nor do they bind the request to the Edge's exact
   current permit slot, `stopRevision`, and effect-boundary high-water at
   companion consumption.

   The Edge can therefore issue a valid `P0` privileged request and leave it
   delayed in IPC. It can then complete its Edge-side transfer `X_E`, permanently
   superseding `P0` locally, while the companion has not yet completed `X_C` and
   still has `P0` in its local active slot. The delayed request still carries a
   valid old `EdgeActivationReceipt`, caller, nonce, activation, and unexpired
   horizon; the companion can accept it after `X_E`, causing a `P0` privileged
   effect precisely while the architecture claims the partial transfer accepts
   neither permit. Replay of a request whose nonce was checked but not durably
   and atomically consumed has the same problem.

   The bounded correction must make every admin privileged boundary use an
   immutable, stable, one-use Edge reservation/request identity bound to the
   exact permit and activation, canonical operation/target/parameters, caller
   and nonce, Edge participant/slot or transfer identity, `stopRevision`, local
   boundary sequence/high-water, and fixed horizon. Companion acceptance must
   atomically verify and consume that exact reservation at its serialized
   effect gate and return the existing receipt on exact replay. Before Edge
   transfer `X_E` may commit, old-slot reservation admission must close and all
   issued `P0` reservations must be durably consumed, rejected/tombstoned,
   expired, or retained as unresolved evidence that blocks the transfer; a
   queued transport write or Edge activation receipt is not drain proof. Crash,
   response loss, delayed delivery, duplicate delivery, and `X_E`/`X_C` races
   must remain no-effect, safely unavailable, or explicitly ambiguous—never a
   hidden predecessor effect. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:619-646,939-999`;
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:167-181`; and
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:27-69,144-159`.

## Findings

### High

1. **The required external `AuthorityAnchor` security root is absent from the
   topology, authority, trust, and recovery contracts.** Architecture 0.1 makes
   externally anchored transition evidence a prerequisite for authority
   activation and effect admission, but does not define the anchor's canonical
   identity, lineage, trust root, authorized writers, verification boundary, or
   recovery behavior. Participants therefore cannot independently distinguish
   a valid current lineage from rollback, fork, restoration, or anchor loss.

   The bounded correction must define one canonical Fleet-scoped, non-forking,
   linearizable append/CAS lineage with a stable anchor identity, genesis/trust
   root, and epoch; scoped authenticated writers; independently verifiable
   receipts; and a pinned participant high-water. It must provide exact-ID
   ambiguity lookup and fail closed during outage, rollback, fork, or loss.
   Anchor replacement must be owner-attended and require a full reset,
   predecessor closure, and participant reenrollment rather than transparent
   failover. G02 defines this mechanism-independent contract; G04 selects the
   concrete mechanism. This does not introduce distributed consensus or an
   alternate authority path. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:76-140,465-494,511-563,604-632,1186-1268,1362-1385,1454-1495`;
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:50-78,271-293`;
   and
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:106-125,271-285`.

### Medium

1. **The generic renewal high-water predicate contradicts the admin
   specialization's required post-preparation drain.** Each `R_i` freezes the
   journal and effect-boundary high-waters while predecessor `P0` remains
   usable. Generic `X_i` then requires those high-waters to remain unchanged,
   while admin `X_C` and `X_E` promise to drain legitimate reservation issue or
   consume transitions that may linearize after `R_i`. On a strict reading any
   such race permanently stales renewal; on a permissive reading there is no
   bounded exception.

   Generic `X_i` must retain the unchanged-high-water rule. Only the
   admin-specialized `X_C` and `X_E` may replace it with proof of a complete,
   contiguous, gap-free delta from the corresponding `R_i` high-water through
   final closure, confined to transitions of the exact precommitted `P0`
   reservation namespace. Any gap, unrelated boundary drift, or unresolved
   effect possibility blocks transfer. Every other identity, proof, stop,
   runtime, horizon, and binding predicate remains unchanged. The final
   high-waters and namespace digest must be bound into the `X_C`/`X_E` receipts
   and final activation. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:618-683`;
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:189-236`;
   and
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:167-206`.

2. **Multiple admin reservation slots can barge around a target safety fence
   and violate its at-most-one effect claim.** At the shared issue and companion
   consume gates, two productive reservations can order as `r1`, safety-fence,
   `r2`; the current one-use slot and namespace rules do not establish durable
   target/conflict-scope stop-pending state that prevents `r2` from reaching
   the effect boundary. A fence can therefore claim at most one already
   linearized unresolved boundary while more than one conflicting slot is
   still able to consume.

   Both issue and consume gates must durably establish and enforce a
   target/conflict-scope `STOP_PENDING` non-barging state. At most one
   already-linearized unresolved boundary may remain, and the
   `SafetyControlFenceReceipt` must bind that state and its exact slot. The state
   and receipt survive crash, retry, and exact replay. Multiple productive
   slots may proceed only sequentially after terminal resolution or when their
   conflict scopes are provably exact and disjoint. References:
   `docs/architecture/baseline-0.1.md:538-547,824-833,1007-1059`;
   `docs/adr/0001-hub-edge-command-and-failure-boundary.md:125-164,330-337`;
   and
   `docs/adr/0004-windows-runtime-storage-and-native-helper.md:29-80,223-229`.
